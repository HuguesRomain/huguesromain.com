I like building. The expensive mistake is building the wrong thing. Signal Radar is a SaaS I made to find exploitable app markets from trend signals, before I commit a month of Expo work to a store listing nobody opens.

## Why a tool, not a spreadsheet

Axiome ships consumer apps. Each one needs a wedge: a job people already try to do, a channel that can reach them, a paywall that is not a costume. Spreadsheets rot. A product that watches signals (searches, competitors, demand that already exists) is something I can reopen on a Monday and trust.

I am not claiming a proprietary oracle. The point is to make “is this a market?” a repeatable step, the same way the [Axiome release chain](/en/blog/shipping-consumer-apps-solo) made “can I submit this week?” a procedure.

## Where it sits

Signal Radar is a personal product, next to Astrotierra. It does not replace talking to users. It stops me from starting from zero every time I want a new app. Belle, KnuckleMax and Koji still had to be designed and shipped; the scanner does not write Swift, and I do not write C++. It answers a narrower question: is there a market worth the studio’s next cycle?

If you want the studio itself, that is [Axiome App Studio](/en/experience/axiome). If you want the agent platform, that is [aiOrigin](/en/experience/aiorigin).
